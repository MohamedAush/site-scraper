* September 3, 2026
* Liquidation
* Reference Number: 28172

Pursuant to Section 246 of Law No. 7/2023 (Companies Act), and upon completion of all requirements stipulated in Regulation No. R-83/2025 (Regulation on the Procedures Determined by the Registrar for the Dissolution of Companies under Section 75(a)(3) of Law No. 10/96 (Companies Act of the Republic of Maldives)), 28 companies have been dissolved, and notice of the dissolution of the said companies is hereby published.

The list of companies dissolved accordingly has been published on the Ministry’s website. ([Link](https://trade.gov.mv/28145/))

For further information or clarification regarding this announcement, please contact the Ministry via email at business@1500help.mv.

03 September 2026
