* January 14, 2024
* Announcement
* Reference Number: 13733

Effective immediately, we are accepting applications for publishing Liquidation Notices in the Registrar’s Gazette.

- All notices must adhere to the prescribed format.
- Requests received after 1400hrs will be published on the next business day.
- Submissions are only accepted from appointed liquidators.
- Submit requests through the [Business Portal](https://business.egov.mv).
- Select “Request to Place a Notice in Registrar’s Gazette” option under General Service Requests.

For timely and accurate publication, please follow these guidelines.
