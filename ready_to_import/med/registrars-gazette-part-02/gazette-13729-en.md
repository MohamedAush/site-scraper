* January 14, 2024
* Announcement
* Reference Number: 13729

We are pleased to announce that, effective 7th January 2024, we have started accepting applications to register single-member companies.

Share transfers for converting existing companies into single-member companies are not yet available.

We anticipate offering share transfer services for conversions once the relevant rules are in place.
